# G-mail authenticator

1. Go to your Google Account security settings:

   * [Google Account Security](https://myaccount.google.com/security?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

2. Under **How you sign in to Google**, select **2-Step Verification**.

   * If it's not enabled yet, follow the prompts to turn it on. You'll typically verify your phone number first.

3. Once 2-Step Verification is enabled, return to the **2-Step Verification** page.

4. Find **Authenticator app** and choose **Set up authenticator**.

5. Google will display a **QR code**.

6. Open your authenticator app on your phone:

   * Google Authenticator
   * Microsoft Authenticator
   * Authy
   * Another TOTP-compatible app

7. In the app, tap **Add account** (usually a **+** button) and **scan the QR code**.

8. The app will generate a **6-digit code**. Enter that code on Google's setup page to confirm.

After setup:

* When signing in, Google may ask for the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
* You can also keep other verification methods (such as security prompts or backup codes) as backup options.

It's also a good idea to generate backup codes:

* On the **2-Step Verification** page, select **Backup codes** and save them somewhere secure in case you lose access to your authenticator app.

If instead you meant an **"App Password"** (an authentication key for an email client like Outlook, Thunderbird, or an older device), let me know what application you're configuring, and I can provide the appropriate steps.
